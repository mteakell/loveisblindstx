Job notes

Two minutes after an install. Only the first two boxes are required, everything else is whatever you can be bothered to type. One good sentence is worth more than a full form.

This page is for the team. It is not linked from the site and it does not appear in search.

This one is gold. Nobody else publishes which neighbourhoods have which window problems.
This is the bit that becomes a blog post. The reasoning matters more than the product.
Real job photos beat stock every time. A phone shot is fine.
Record a voice memo on the drive to the next job and send it. Same value, no typing.

No customer names or addresses unless they have said yes to being mentioned.

If you have five minutes instead of two

Pick any one of these and answer it in a couple of sentences. Each one is a question customers actually search for and that only somebody doing the work can answer.

Local knowledge (highest value)
  • Which subdivisions or streets in your territory have window problems you see over and over?
  • Are there HOA rules in any neighbourhood you serve that limit what people can install?
  • Which builders in your area used window sizes or frame depths that cause trouble later?
  • What is different about older homes versus new build in your territory?
  • Which month of the year do people finally call about afternoon sun, and why then?
The job itself
  • Describe the last install where the customer asked for one thing and you recommended another.
  • What is the most common measuring or spec mistake you find on treatments someone else installed?
  • What did you have to solve on site last week that was not in the plan?
  • What is a job you talked a customer out of, and what did you do instead?
  • What is the fastest way you can tell a window is going to be a problem?
What customers get wrong
  • What do people most often misunderstand about blackout?
  • What do they misunderstand about privacy after dark?
  • What do they think costs a lot that does not, and vice versa?
  • What do people buy that they regret within two years?
  • What question do you get asked on almost every consultation?
Patio and exterior
  • What do people not realise about exterior shades until you explain it?
  • How do you decide openness factor on a specific patio?
  • What goes wrong on patios that were done by someone else?
  • What is the wind conversation you have with every patio customer?
Product reality
  • Which product do you install most, and which do you personally rate highest?
  • What holds up worst in Texas sun, in your actual experience?
  • What is a product people ask for that you steer them away from?
  • What has changed in the last two years that customers do not know about yet?
