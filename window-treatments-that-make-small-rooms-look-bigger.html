Window Treatments That Make Small Rooms Look Bigger

TLDR;
To make a small room look bigger, choose light-colored, sheer, or low-profile window treatments like cellular shades, roller shades, or floor-to-ceiling drapes. These options enhance natural light, create visual height and width, and reduce clutter for a more open and airy feel.

Small Room Window Ideas That Work

At Love Is Blinds TX, we often help clients transform tight, cramped rooms into visually larger, more breathable spaces,without knocking down walls. The secret? The right window treatments.

Small spaces depend heavily on visual tricks, and your window coverings can make or break that illusion. If your goal is to make a room feel bigger, brighter, and more breathable, the way you dress your windows matters just as much as your furniture or paint color.

This post explores the most effective stylish window coverings to visually expand your small room,backed by design principles, product insights, and Texas-specific considerations.

Why Window Treatments Matter in Small Spaces

Window treatments aren't just decorative,they're spatial influencers.

Here’s what the right ones do:

  • Maximize natural light , making the room feel brighter and more open

  • Draw the eye upward or outward , adding height or width to the space

  • Minimize visual clutter , making everything feel sleeker and more cohesive

  • Offer thermal efficiency , especially important in hot Texas climates

Stat to consider: Hanging curtains high and wide can increase the visual height of your ceiling by up to 15%.

Mounting Techniques That Maximize Space

It’s not just what you hang,it’s how you hang it.

Try these expert mounting tricks:

  • Hang high & wide

  • Mount curtain rods 4–6 inches above the window frame

  • Extend rods 8–12 inches beyond the sides of the window

  • This gives the illusion of a larger window and taller ceiling

  • Wall-to-wall rods

  • A single rod that spans an entire wall opens the width of the room

  • Use slim hardware

  • Opt for minimal brackets, rings, or flush-mounted rods

  • Avoid chunky finials or ornate hardware that feels heavy

  • Recess mount where possible

  • Installing shades or blinds inside the window frame preserves space and creates clean lines

Best Window Treatments for Small Rooms

These styles have been tested, loved, and recommended by window treatment experts like us at Love Is Blinds TX:

Sheer Curtains


  • Allow natural light to flow through

  • Visually soften the room

  • Offer daytime privacy without blocking brightness

  • Great for layering with heavier treatments

Cellular (Honeycomb) Shades


  • Ultra-slim, clean appearance when raised

  • Provide insulation,perfect for Texas heat

  • Come in light-filtering and blackout options

  • Available in neutral tones that don’t overpower

Roller Shades


  • Discreet and compact when rolled up

  • Ideal for minimalist, small rooms

  • Easy to motorize for convenience

  • Pair well with side panels for style without bulk

Roman Shades


  • Neat folds add soft texture without overwhelming

  • Can be mounted inside or outside the frame

  • Customizable patterns and fabrics

  • Stylish in offices, powder rooms, or cozy bedrooms

Plantation Shutters


  • Reflect light and add structure

  • Built-in look that minimizes visual clutter

  • Durable in high-moisture areas like bathrooms

  • Energy-efficient for controlling heat gain﻿

Blinds vs. Curtains: Which Is Better for Small Spaces?

It depends on your goals.

Blinds:

  • Take up less physical and visual space

  • Ideal for narrow or deep-set windows

  • Offer precise light control

Curtains:

  • Add softness and flow

  • When hung properly (high and wide), they make windows look larger

  • Offer better acoustic dampening

Tip: For maximum flexibility, layer both,mount a blind inside the frame and sheer curtains outside for form and function.

Layered Window Treatments for Space Illusion

Layering isn’t just stylish,it’s strategic.

Base + overlay = depth + control:

  • Base: Roller, cellular, or Roman shade

  • Overlay: Sheer or lightweight curtain panel

  • Optional third layer: Decorative drapes or blackout side panels

Benefits:

  • Total light control throughout the day

  • Enhanced privacy

  • More texture = more visual interest without crowding

  • Lets you adjust function without changing the whole look

Color, Pattern & Visual Tricks That Fool the Eye

Color and design can trick the eye,and that’s a good thing.

What works best:

  • Light neutrals: White, cream, pale gray, beige,these reflect light

  • Avoid dark or heavy prints: These absorb light and visually “close in” the space

  • Vertical stripes: Elongate the room and draw the eye upward

  • Horizontal patterns: Widen a narrow wall or small window

  • Monochrome layers: Multiple tones of the same color can create depth without distraction

Extra tip: Match curtains with the wall color to blur boundaries and expand the perceived space.

Texas-Specific Window Treatment Considerations

Small rooms in Texas face extra challenges: heat, humidity, and brightness.

Smart solutions for our local climate:

  • Energy-efficient cellular shades: Trap air and reduce heat gain

  • Moisture-resistant shutters or faux wood blinds: Great for humid zones like bathrooms or coastal areas

  • Motorized roller shades with timers or sensors: Open in the morning for natural light, close midday to block heat

  • Blackout liners for bedrooms: Control glare and maintain sleep quality, even during scorching summers

  • UV-filtering sheers: Protect furniture and floors while keeping the room bright

Budget-Friendly Fixes That Still Work

You don’t need a huge budget to make a small room feel larger.

Try these cost-effective options:

  • Thrift lightweight drapes and bleach to lighten color

  • DIY your curtain rod higher using wood dowels or PVC pipe

  • Layer ready-made sheers with an inexpensive roller shade

  • Use removable adhesive hardware if you’re renting

  • Repurpose drapes as a canopy or room divider for multifunctional illusion﻿

How to Install for Maximum Impact

Getting the size and placement right is just as important as choosing the treatment.

Measure like a pro:

  • Rod height: 4–6 inches above frame

  • Rod width: Extend 8–12 inches beyond window on both sides

  • Curtain length: Always floor-length (puddled or just grazing)

Installation tips:

  • Use a level to avoid crooked mounting

  • Drill into wall studs for heavy treatments

  • Secure cords to prevent child hazards

  • Use moisture-safe anchors in bathrooms﻿

Frequently Asked Questions

Can small rooms look bigger without curtains?

Yes, but curtains hung properly can enhance the effect. Opt for sleek shades or inside-mount blinds if you prefer a no-fabric look.

What’s the best window treatment for small bedrooms?

Light-filtering cellular or roller shades paired with floor-length sheer panels.

Should curtains touch the floor?

Absolutely,curtains should either graze or pool at the floor to add vertical stretch.

How do you layer blinds and drapes?

Mount the blind inside the frame and the drapes outside. Keep layers light and cohesive in color.

Do shutters make rooms feel smaller?

Not if used correctly. Choose white or light-colored plantation shutters that reflect light and fit snugly within the window frame.
