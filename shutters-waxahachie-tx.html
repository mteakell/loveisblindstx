What is the difference between plantation shutters and other shutters?
Plantation shutters have wide louvers and are the most common interior style. Cafe shutters cover the lower half, solid panels have no louvers at all, and specialty shapes are built to a template. All are interior shutters.
Do you do exterior shutters?
Our work is interior shutters. If what you want is heat control on the outside of the glass, exterior patio shades or solar screens do that job far better than a decorative exterior shutter.
Can you shutter an arched window?
Yes. Arches, angles and circles are templated on site and built individually, which is why we measure them ourselves rather than working from your numbers.
Which rooms suit shutters best?
Front rooms and anywhere with good trim, because shutters are read as part of the house. Bathrooms want composite rather than wood.
Which shutter style blocks the most light?
Solid panels, because there are no louvers and therefore no gaps. A plantation shutter with closed louvers is next.
