Window treatments for Irving homes

No window treatment soundproofs a room, but the right one makes a noticeable difference. Hard glass reflects sound into a room; fabric and trapped air soften it. In bedrooms near the flight paths or busy roads, that is often the first thing Irving homeowners ask about.

We look at each room's windows, how you use it, and how much darkness it needs along with quiet, then show you the fabrics and constructions that do the most.

Honeycomb shades

Cellular shades for quiet and insulation

Cellular shades fold into cells that hold air against the glass. That air slows heat and softens sound. Double cell construction adds a second layer of cells and does more of both.

Drapery over a shade

For the quietest and darkest bedroom, layer full drapery over a working shade. The fabric softens echo inside the room, and the drapery closes the light gaps at the edges of the shade.

Glare on a screen and a bright window behind you on camera are the two problems in a home office. A light filtering or solar shade fixes both without darkening the room.

Questions

Irving window treatment questions

Do window treatments reduce airplane noise?

They soften it. Fabric and the air in cellular shades absorb some sound, and drapery over a shade does the most. Nothing makes a room soundproof.

Which shade is quietest?

Double cell cellular shades, especially with drapery layered over them.

How do I get a bedroom completely dark?

Blackout fabric plus a way to close the edges: side channels, an outside mount with overlap, or drapery.
