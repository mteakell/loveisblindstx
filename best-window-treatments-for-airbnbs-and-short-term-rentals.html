Best Window Treatments for Airbnbs and

Short-Term Rentals

TLDR;
The best window treatments for Airbnbs and short-term rentals offer a perfect balance of privacy, light control, style, and durability. Choose blackout options for bedrooms, easy-to-clean faux wood blinds for kitchens and baths, and energy-efficient shades throughout. For safety and convenience, opt for cordless or smart solutions.

Why Window Treatments Matter in Short-Term Rentals

Window treatments aren’t just decorative,they directly impact guest satisfaction, privacy, comfort, and even your reviews.

According to a recent survey, 72% of guests mention lighting or privacy in reviews, often referencing the quality (or lack thereof) of window coverings.

Here's why your Airbnb’s window treatments matter:

  • Guest Experience: Proper treatments enhance sleep quality, temperature regulation, and ambiance.
  • Privacy and Security: Guests want to feel safe and unseen,especially at night.
  • First Impressions: Modern, clean blinds elevate your listing’s visual appeal and lead to better bookings.
  • Energy Efficiency: Insulated treatments reduce heating/cooling costs,important if you cover utilities.
  • Reviews and ROI: Thoughtful upgrades like window treatments often show up in 5-star feedback.

How to Choose the Right Window Treatments for Rentals

Durability, Maintenance & Safety


For high-turnover properties, durability and ease of maintenance are non-negotiable.

Choose materials like:

  • Faux wood blinds – moisture-resistant, long-lasting, and wipeable.
  • Polyester or microfiber curtains – machine washable and wrinkle-resistant.
  • Vinyl roller shades – durable and minimalistic, ideal for tighter budgets.

Safety tips for hosts:

  • Use cordless options to eliminate strangulation hazards.
  • Avoid dangling pull cords or outdated tension systems.
  • Prioritize child-safe blinds if your listing is family-friendly.

Light Control & Privacy


Light and privacy control should match the room’s function.

Top options for specific needs:

  • Blackout shades – perfect for bedrooms to ensure quality sleep.
  • Top-down/bottom-up shades – give privacy without blocking natural light.
  • Sheer curtains layered with heavier drapes – flexible lighting options with style.
  • Solar shades – reduce glare in living spaces while maintaining views.

Guests want to wake up on their terms. Invest in solutions that provide flexibility.

Style & Aesthetic Compatibility


Your window treatments should reflect your overall interior style while remaining functional.

Popular design directions:

  • Minimalist spaces – roller shades or cellular shades in neutral tones.
  • Modern farmhouse – layered linen curtains or natural woven wood shades.
  • Coastal themes – white faux wood blinds or airy sheers.
  • Urban loft – solar shades or motorized blackout panels.

Choose styles that photograph well, complement your color scheme, and boost the visual appeal of your listing.

Room-by-Room Window Treatment Recommendations

Bedroom


The bedroom is where privacy and darkness matter most.

Best choices:

  • Blackout curtains or cellular shades – ideal for blocking light and reducing noise.
  • Cordless blackout roller shades – sleek, modern, and safe for kids.
  • Dual-layer options – blackout at night, sheer by day.

Living Room


Guests gather here, so strike a balance between privacy and aesthetics.

Top picks:

  • Solar shades – block UV without cutting off natural light.
  • Light-filtering blinds – offer flexible light control without total darkness.
  • Layered drapes and sheers – elevate the space while giving guests control.

Bathroom & Kitchen


These rooms require moisture-resistant, easy-to-clean options like the best blinds for short-term rentals.

Recommended treatments:

  • Faux wood blinds – resist humidity and splashes.
  • Vinyl roller shades – simple and mold-resistant.
  • Café curtains – provide privacy while letting in light.

Patio Doors / Sliding Glass


These can be tricky, but functionality is key.

Best solutions:

  • Panel track shades – easy to glide and modern looking.
  • Vertical cellular shades – insulate while offering full coverage.
  • Sheer verticals – soft look with light control.

Layering for Comfort and Aesthetics

Layering isn’t just for looks,it’s a strategy.

Why layer your treatments?

  • Function + beauty: Combine blackout curtains with sheers for dual use.
  • Better insulation: Add thermal curtains for warmth in colder climates.
  • Visual texture: Use different fabrics for a high-end appearance.

Examples of layering:

  • Double curtain rods: blackout panel + sheer curtain
  • Roman shade + drapes: adds depth and control
  • Solar shade + linen drapes: modern and soft

Installation Tips: DIY or Pro?

Step-by-Step DIY Guide


Installing your own blinds can save money and time if you're handy.

Essentials:

  • Use a measuring tape,measure twice, install once.
  • Mount brackets securely to studs or use anchors.
  • Align blinds evenly for a polished look.
  • For temporary fixes, tension rods or adhesive-mounted shades can work.

Tools you’ll need:

  • Drill or screwdriver
  • Level
  • Brackets and screws
  • Pencil for marking

When to Hire a Pro


Consider a professional installer if:

  • You have multiple windows or complex bay windows.
  • You want motorized or smart shades installed properly.
  • Your time is limited and your budget allows it.

Hiring a pro ensures precise installation and avoids costly mistakes.

Sustainable and Smart Window Treatments

Today’s guests appreciate sustainability and tech.

Eco-Friendly Options:

  • Bamboo shades – renewable, stylish, and durable.
  • Recycled polyester curtains – low-impact, washable, modern.

Smart Window Treatments:

  • Motorized blinds – adjust remotely or via schedule.
  • App-controlled shades – connect with Alexa, Google Home.
  • Solar-powered options – energy-saving and futuristic.

These upgrades make your Airbnb feel modern and high-end.

Budgeting, ROI & Value for Hosts

Window treatments are a smart investment,not just a cost.

Budget tips:

  • Use roller shades or faux wood for economy and longevity.
  • Buy in bulk or during sales for discounts.
  • Stick to neutral colors for future flexibility.

ROI factors:

  • Improved sleep = better reviews
  • Clean look = higher booking rates
  • Energy savings = lower bills
  • Smart upgrades = standout listings

Every dollar spent should improve the guest experience, which translates directly to your bottom line.

Maintenance and Cleaning Best Practices

Clean window coverings are non-negotiable for short-term rentals.

Tips by type:

  • Blinds: Dust weekly with microfiber or use a vacuum brush.
  • Curtains: Wash monthly (check care tag) or steam clean in place.
  • Roller shades: Wipe with damp cloth.
  • Wood/faux wood: Spot clean with mild soap and water.

Pro tip: Keep a checklist for turnovers to ensure every treatment is fresh for the next guest.

FAQs

What are the best blinds for guest privacy?

Blackout cellular or roller shades paired with curtains offer full privacy.

Can I use curtains in an Airbnb?

Yes, especially when layered for function and design impact.

How do I keep window treatments safe for kids?

Use cordless or motorized treatments to avoid hazards.

What’s the best solution for sliding doors?

Panel track or vertical cellular shades for coverage and style.

Do window treatments affect Airbnb ratings?

Yes,privacy, comfort, and style often influence reviews and repeat bookings.
