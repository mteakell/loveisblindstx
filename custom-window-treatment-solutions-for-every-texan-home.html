FAQ's

  • Q: What are the best window treatment ideas for bay windows in Texas?

    A: Bay windows are architectural highlights of any home, offering panoramic views and unique design opportunities. In Texas, where light control and privacy are crucial due to the intense sun, custom-fitted shades or blinds for each section of the bay window are ideal. These treatments allow for precise control over light, helping to manage heat and protect interiors from UV exposure while maintaining privacy. 


    Motorized options add a layer of convenience, allowing you to adjust settings with the push of a button or even remotely, which is particularly beneficial for windows in hard-to-reach places or for adding smart home capabilities.


  • Q: Can window treatments for arched windows be customized in Texas?

    A: Yes, window treatments for arched windows can be fully customized to fit their unique shape and architectural style. Tailoring these treatments ensures they align perfectly with the window's contours, enhancing both the window's inherent beauty and the overall room's aesthetics. 


    Options include sunburst shutters, which radiate out from the center, mimicking the shape of the arch, or custom-fit shades that can be crafted to the exact arch specifications. These options ensure that the functional aspects of light control and privacy are met without undermining the visual appeal of these striking windows.


  • Q: What solutions are effective for managing light in rooms with skylights in Texas?

    A: Managing light in rooms with skylights is particularly important in Texas, where the sun can be both a blessing and a challenge. Motorized skylight shades are highly effective in this role, providing the ability to adjust the amount of sunlight entering through these high windows effortlessly. These shades can be integrated with smart home systems for automated adjustments based on the time of day or the intensity of the sun. 


    Additionally, they help in reducing heat gain during the hottest parts of the day and provide UV protection to prevent fading of furniture and floors.


  • Q: What are suitable window coverings for large picture windows in Texas?

    A: For large picture windows, which often serve as the centerpiece of a room, finding the right balance between preserving views and managing light and privacy is key. Panel track blinds and substantial draperies are excellent choices for these windows. Panel track blinds offer a modern look and are easy to operate, sliding smoothly to cover or uncover large expanses of glass as needed. 


    Draperies can be made from fabrics that provide thermal insulation and sound absorption, enhancing energy efficiency and reducing noise, while also adding a touch of elegance and drama to the room. Both options allow for versatility in design and functionality, making them ideal for the expansive views and varied climate of Texas.
