Plantation Shutters for Montgomery homes

Water reflects light up into a room at a low, bright angle. With shutters you set the louvers to meet it: tilted one way for the reflection, the other way for the overhead sun, or opened flat with the panels swung back when you want to see the water clearly. Every frame is built to the measured opening, including wide lake-facing windows.

Bedrooms that face the lake get the earliest light, and closing the louvers holds most of it back. For a porch room or sunroom off the water, composite shutters handle the humidity. We bring samples to the free in-home consultation and match the finish to your trim.

Plantation Shutters questions from Montgomery

Do plantation shutters help with lake glare in Montgomery?

Yes, because the louvers adjust to the angle of the light. Tilt them to block the reflection off the water, and open them when you want the full view of the lake.

What shutters suit a sunroom near Lake Conroe?

Composite, since it is moisture-proof and handles the humidity near the water. We measure each opening in the room on site, and every panel is built to that size.

Do you install plantation shutters in Montgomery?

Yes. Zeke Lee runs Love Is Blinds North Houston, which covers Montgomery along with Magnolia, Tomball, The Woodlands, Spring, Conroe, Willis, Waller and the communities between them. The team that measures your windows installs them. Call (281) 864-3309.

Will shutters fit my windows?

Almost always, but the frame style depends on what the opening allows. Every shutter needs a minimum depth for the louvers to rotate. If the opening is shallow, the answer is a frame that projects rather than abandoning shutters.

Do shutters add value to a home?

They stay with the house, so buyers read them as part of the property rather than a covering that will be replaced. That is why people put shutters in the rooms that show and blinds or shades elsewhere.

Real wood or composite?

Wood is lighter and takes stain beautifully, so it suits dry living areas. Composite handles moisture and hard sun without warping, which wins in bathrooms, kitchens and west facing walls.

How much do plantation shutters cost in Montgomery?

It depends on the size of each opening, the material and the options you choose. We measure every opening on site and give you a written quote with installation included. The consultation is free.
