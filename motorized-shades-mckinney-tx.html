What moves the price of motorized shades

  • The treatment itself, since a motor moves whatever you put on the window
  • Width and weight, which decide motor sizing on large spans
  • Whether you want a hub, wall switches, or app and voice control
  • How many openings you motorize, since it is priced per opening
  • Power: battery, hardwired or solar charged

We measure the openings and quote from those measurements. The number on your estimate is the number you pay.

Motorized Shades questions from McKinney

Do you install motorized shades in McKinney?
Yes. Jake Wade and Jonathan Arosemena runs Love Is Blinds North Texas, which covers McKinney along with Sherman, Celina, Plano, McKinney, Denton and the northern suburbs. The person who measures your windows is the person who fits them. Call (940) 291-8144.
Battery, hardwired or solar?
Battery is the simplest retrofit and needs no electrician. Hardwired is best during a remodel. Solar suits windows that get real sun, which in Texas is most of them.
Will it work with my smart home?
Tell us which system you run at the consultation and we confirm compatibility before you order rather than after. We pair and program everything at installation.
What if a motor needs reprogramming later?
You call the same team that installed it. That is the practical difference between buying motorization locally and buying it online.
How long does a battery last?
It depends on how often the shade moves and how heavy it is. Solar charging removes the question on any window that gets real sun.
Can you motorize shades I already have?
Sometimes, where the existing hardware allows it. We check at the consultation rather than promising it in advance.
How much do motorized shades cost in McKinney?
It depends on the size of the openings, the material and the options you choose. We measure every opening on site and quote from those measurements, so the number on your estimate is the number you pay. The consultation is free.
