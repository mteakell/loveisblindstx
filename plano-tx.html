Plantation shutters

Plantation shutters as the main update

Plantation shutters change how a house reads, inside and from the street. They tilt to control light, close to insulate, and are built in their own frame so an opening that has shifted over the years still gets a clean fit. Hardwood for living and dining rooms, composite where there is humidity.

Roman shades

Roman shades for softer rooms

In dining rooms and bedrooms, a roman shade adds fabric and texture that blinds and shutters do not. Flat folds read tailored; relaxed folds suit traditional rooms. Liners decide whether the shade filters light, adds privacy or darkens the room.

Entries and sidelights

Narrow sidelights beside a front door are often left bare. Shutters built for the sidelights give privacy at the entry and match the rest of the front of the house.

A Plano upgrade, front to back

Planning the house

Every plan starts at your windows, with samples in your own light. For more detail, read Signs it is time to replace your blinds.

Plan yours with us
  • Front windows and entry: plantation shutters, including the sidelights.
  • Patio door: panel track shades where verticals used to hang.
  • Dining room and bedrooms: roman shades with the liner each room needs.
  • Kitchen and baths: faux wood blinds or composite shutters.

Keeping one white or one stain across every room keeps the house consistent from the street.

Questions

Plano window treatment questions

Will you take down the old blinds in my Plano home?

Yes. The old treatments come down on install day and leave with us if you want them gone.

Hardwood or composite shutters?

Hardwood for display rooms; composite for kitchens, baths and anywhere with humidity.

Can sidelights get shutters?

Yes, built to the sidelight so they match the front windows.

Where is your Plano office?

1400 Preston Rd, Suite 477, Plano, TX 75093. Consultations happen at your home.

Should shutters and shades match across the house?

Keeping them in one color family looks consistent from outside, even when the products change room to room.

How much do window treatments cost in Plano?

It depends on the size of each opening, the material and the options you choose. We measure every opening on site and give you a written quote with installation included. The consultation is free.
