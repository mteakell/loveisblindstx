Common questions about blinds installation.

Inside mount or outside mount - which is better?
Inside mount looks cleaner and shows off the frame; outside mount covers more light and hides shallow or damaged frames. We check your frame depth during the free consultation and recommend per window.
Can you install blinds on doors and sliders?
Yes - vertical blinds and specialty door-mounted blinds are routine. Sliders often do even better with panel track shades, which we'll show you as an option.
Do you fix blinds that were installed badly by someone else?
We generally replace rather than repair other companies' installs - a badly measured blind can't be re-mounted into a good one. We'll give you an honest assessment either way.
