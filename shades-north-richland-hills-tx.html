What is openness factor?
The percentage of the weave that is open. Lower blocks more sun and gives more daytime privacy but softens the view. Higher keeps the view and still cuts glare.
Can shades be blackout?
Yes, but an inside mount always leaks light at the edges. Real darkness needs an outside mount with overlap, side channels, or a drape over the top.
Can shades be cleaned?
Vacuum brush for dust, a barely damp cloth for marks, and leave them fully down until dry. Rolling a damp shade is how you get mildew.
Do cellular shades really save energy?
They slow heat transfer at the glass, which is measurable on a hot wall. They are not insulation in the way a wall is, but on a west bedroom the difference is noticeable.
What is top down bottom up?
A shade that opens from the top as well as the bottom, so you can take daylight in high while the lower half stays closed.
