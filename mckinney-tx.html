Window treatments for McKinney homes

The windows that give a McKinney home its character are the hard ones to cover: arches, bays, tall narrow windows and openings that have shifted over the years. Stock treatments either skip the shape or cover it badly.

Our North Texas team measures every opening on site, including the shapes, and recommends the treatment that follows each one.

Arched and specialty windows

Arched and specialty windows

Shutters can be built to follow an arched top, so the shape stays part of the room instead of being covered by a square treatment. Where a shade suits the room better, it can cover the rectangular part of the window and leave the arch open.

Roman shades for character rooms

Roman shades bring fabric, pattern and softness to dining rooms, bedrooms and bays. Each shade is built to its own window, so the folds land level across a bay.

Bedrooms with a row of windows

A bedroom with several windows in a row needs every shade to line up. Building each to its measured opening keeps the bottoms level when they are lowered.

Questions

McKinney window treatment questions

Can you cover an arched window in McKinney?

Yes. Shutters can follow the arch, or a shade can cover the rectangular part and leave the arch open.

Do roman shades work in a bay window?

Yes. Each shade is built to its own window so the folds land level.

Do roman shades come blackout?

Yes, with a blackout liner.

Do you serve homes near downtown McKinney?

Yes. Our North Texas team serves all of McKinney.

Do arched windows cost more?

Special shapes are built to the opening, which is part of what the written quote covers. We measure them on site.

How much do window treatments cost in McKinney?

It depends on the size of each opening, the material and the options you choose. We measure every opening on site and give you a written quote with installation included. The consultation is free.
