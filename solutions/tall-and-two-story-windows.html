Window Treatments for Tall and Two-Story Windows

Two-story great rooms and tall glass bring in the most light in a house, and the most heat. Covering them comes down to reach, scale, and how the wall looks from both floors. Motorized shades handle the height, and matching fabrics keep the whole wall reading as one design.

Common questions

How do you open shades on two-story windows?
With motorization. Upper shades run from a remote, app, schedule or voice assistant, so they move with the sun instead of staying in one position.
What is the best window treatment for a two-story great room in Texas?
Motorized roller or solar shades on the upper glass, often with shutters or cellular shades on the lower tier you can reach.
Can motorized shades be added without new wiring?
Yes. Battery-powered motors retrofit without wiring.
