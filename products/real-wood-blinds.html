    Where real wood earns its place

    Living rooms, formal dining rooms, studies, and bedrooms - anywhere away from steam and splash. Real wood slats are lighter than faux, which means larger windows lift easier and the stack is smaller when raised. Stains range from pale natural tones to deep espresso, matched against your trim during the in-home consultation.

    Custom-stained, custom-measured

    Each set is built to your exact opening and finished to complement your trim and floors. Pair real wood up front with faux wood in the wet rooms and the whole house reads consistent - at a smarter total price.

    Why order through Love Is Blinds?

    • Genuine hardwood grain and warmth no imitation fully matches.
    • Lighter slats - better for wide windows and easier daily operation.
    • Custom stains and paints matched to your trim in your own light.

    Questions homeowners ask about real wood blinds.

    Where should I avoid real wood blinds?
    Bathrooms, kitchens, and laundry rooms - humidity can warp and crack natural wood over time. Use faux wood there and save real wood for the dry rooms where its warmth shows.
    Do real wood blinds block light well?
    Closed, they block most light; for true blackout, we pair them with a drapery or shade layer. The consultation covers what each room needs.
    Are real wood blinds worth the price difference?
    In display rooms, usually yes - the grain and warmth read clearly. In wet or high-traffic rooms, faux wood is the smarter spend. That honest mix is what we quote most.
