Why shutters hold value

Blinds and shades are furnishings; shutters are millwork. They convey with the house, read as a permanent finish in listing photos, and survive decades of daily use. In established Texas neighborhoods, front-of-house shutters are close to expected.

Built to the window you actually have

Arches, transoms, out-of-square historic frames - shutters are custom-built panel by panel, which is why the in-home measurement matters. Painted or stained to match your existing trim so they read original to the house.

Questions homeowners ask about custom shutters.

Are shutters worth the higher price?
For front-of-house and long-stay homes, usually yes - they outlast every other treatment and return value at resale. For rooms where budget matters more, we'll say so and spec something smarter.
What's the difference between shutters and plantation shutters?
Plantation shutters are the wide-louver style - 2.5″ to 4.5″ louvers that suit modern and traditional homes alike. It's what nearly everyone means by 'shutters' today, and it's what we build.
How long do custom shutters take?
They're the most custom product we sell, so figure the longer end of two to five weeks from order to install. The timeline goes in writing before anything is ordered.
