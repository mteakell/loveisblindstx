Questions homeowners ask about custom window shades.

What's the difference between all the shade types?
Rollers are clean and versatile; honeycombs insulate; woven woods add texture; dual shades combine two opacities in one; panel tracks cover sliders and wide glass. The in-home consultation exists precisely to sort this out for your actual rooms.
Which shades are best for energy savings?
Honeycomb (cellular) shades, by a wide margin - the trapped air pocket at the glass slows heat transfer in both directions. In Texas's summers they pay for themselves in HVAC relief.
Can I see the fabrics before I order?
Yes - that's the whole point of the free in-home consultation. Real fabric samples, held up in your own light, against your own paint and trim.
