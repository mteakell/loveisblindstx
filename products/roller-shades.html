Questions homeowners ask about roller shades.

What's the difference between roller blinds and roller shades?
Same product family - a fabric panel on a roller tube. 'Blinds' technically have adjustable slats; rollers are shades. Whatever you call them, we custom-fit them.
Do blackout roller shades really black out a room?
The fabric itself blocks essentially all light; the edges are what leak. We minimize gaps with precise measurement, and for media rooms and nurseries we can add side channels for true darkness.
Are solar shades worth it for a west-facing room?
Usually, yes - they knock down glare and heat at the glass while keeping your view, which is exactly the problem a hard western exposure creates in a Texas summer.
