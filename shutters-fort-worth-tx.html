Window Shutters questions from Fort Worth

What louver size should my Fort Worth shutters have?

Shutters come in a range of louver widths. We bring samples to the in-home consultation and help you choose a size that suits each window and the style of the room.

Do shutters add value to a Fort Worth home?

Shutters are treated as millwork. They convey with the house, show as a permanent finish in listing photos, and are the window treatment buyers tend to notice.

Do you install window shutters in Fort Worth?

Yes. Durrell Glick runs Love Is Blinds DFW, which covers Fort Worth along with Denton, the Mid-Cities, Southlake, Grapevine, Coppell and Granbury. The team that measures your windows installs them. Call (817) 617-8826.

What is the difference between plantation shutters and other shutters?

Plantation shutters have wide louvers and are the most common interior style. Cafe shutters cover the lower half, solid panels have no louvers at all, and specialty shapes are built to a template. All are interior shutters.

Do you do exterior shutters?

Our work is interior shutters. If what you want is heat control on the outside of the glass, exterior patio shades or solar screens do that job far better than a decorative exterior shutter.

Can you shutter an arched window?

Yes. Arches, angles and circles are templated on site and built individually, which is why we measure them ourselves rather than working from your numbers.

How much do window shutters cost in Fort Worth?

It depends on the size of each opening, the material and the options you choose. We measure every opening on site and give you a written quote with installation included. The consultation is free.
