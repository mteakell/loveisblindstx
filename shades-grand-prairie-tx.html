Do cellular shades really save energy?
They slow heat transfer at the glass, which is measurable on a hot wall. They are not insulation in the way a wall is, but on a west bedroom the difference is noticeable.
What is top down bottom up?
A shade that opens from the top as well as the bottom, so you can take daylight in high while the lower half stays closed.
Can I layer shades with drapes?
Yes, and it is what most bedrooms end up with. The shade handles light, the drape handles the edges and softens the room.
Which shade is best for a west facing window?
A solar screen shade with a tighter openness factor, or a cellular shade if heat rather than glare is the problem. We look at orientation before recommending anything.
Do solar shades give privacy at night?
No. A weave that gives complete daytime privacy goes transparent once it is dark outside and the lights are on inside. Windows that need to work after dark need blackout or a layered treatment.
