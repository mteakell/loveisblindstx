Window treatments for Justin homes

The trouble shows up when each window is bought on its own: a roller shade in the living room, a different white blind over the sink, verticals on the back door. Standing at the front door you see all of it at once, and none of it matches.

Honeycomb shades

One color family for the main floor

The simplest way to tie an open plan together is one color across every window you can see from the middle of the room. The products can still differ, with a cellular shade in the dining area, a roller shade on the TV wall and a woven shade in the kitchen, all in tones that sit together. Level bottoms and matching headrails finish it.

Woven wood shades

The kitchen window

The window over the sink gets steam and splashes, so it needs a material that cleans easily. Woven wood works if it sits clear of the splash zone and the kitchen has a natural look. Faux wood blinds handle the wettest spots. Either one can be chosen in a tone that matches the living room.

Plantation shutters

Bedrooms can go their own way

Upstairs and down the hall, the bedrooms are out of sight of the main floor, so each can follow what it needs. A nursery may want blackout fabric, a child's room may want shutters that stand up to years of use, and a guest room may suit a lined roman shade.

Questions

Justin window treatment questions

Should every window in an open floor plan match?

The ones you can see together should share a color family. The products can differ by room.

What goes on the back slider in an open plan?

Usually a panel track shade in the same color as the rest of the main floor. It stacks to one side and clears the walkway.

Can the kitchen have a different shade from the living room?

Yes. A woven wood shade or a faux wood blind in a matching tone keeps the look together.

Do you serve Justin?

Yes. Our DFW team measures and installs in Justin, and the in-home consultation costs nothing.

Can I do the main floor now and the bedrooms later?

Yes. One written quote can cover the whole house and be ordered in stages.

How much do window treatments cost in Justin?

It depends on the size of each opening, the material and the options you choose. We measure every opening on site and give you a written quote with installation included. The consultation is free.
