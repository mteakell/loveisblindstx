Window Shutters questions from Hurst

Do you install window shutters in Hurst?
Yes. Durrell Glick runs Love Is Blinds DFW, which covers Hurst along with Fort Worth, Denton, the Mid-Cities, Southlake, Grapevine, Coppell and Granbury. The person who measures your windows is the person who fits them. Call (817) 813-8846.
Can you shutter an arched window?
Yes. Arches, angles and circles are templated on site and built individually, which is why we measure them ourselves rather than working from your numbers.
Which rooms suit shutters best?
Front rooms and anywhere with good trim, because shutters are read as part of the house. Bathrooms want composite rather than wood.
Which shutter style blocks the most light?
Solid panels, because there are no louvers and therefore no gaps. A plantation shutter with closed louvers is next.
Can I shutter a door?
Yes. Door and sidelight panels are built slim with hardware that clears the handle so the door still works normally.
Do shutters work on very wide windows?
Split into multiple panels, or on a sliding track for the widest openings. A single wide panel is not the answer.
How much do window shutters cost in Hurst?
It depends on the size of the openings, the material and the options you choose. We measure every opening on site and quote from those measurements, so the number on your estimate is the number you pay. The consultation is free.
