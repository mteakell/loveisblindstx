Custom Drapery for Irving homes

In an Irving bedroom, a blackout drapery panel layered with a sheer gives you a dark room at night and soft light by day. Mounted outside and past the frame, it closes over the shade beneath it. We plan the shade and the drapery in one visit and price both with installation included.

Custom Drapery questions from Irving

What drapery helps an Irving bedroom stay dark?

A blackout panel, usually layered with a sheer and hung over a shade. The drapery covers the edges of the window, and the shade adds a second layer of light control.

Can you install drapery in an Irving home office?

Yes, a sheer panel for daytime light and a heavier panel for calls is a common pairing. We measure the window on site and hang both on hardware sized for the wall.

Do you install custom drapery in Irving?

Yes. Jake Wade and Jonathan Arosemena run Love Is Blinds North Texas, which covers Irving along with Dallas, Highland Park, Las Colinas, Plano, Frisco, McKinney, Prosper, Celina, Sherman and the surrounding suburbs. The team that measures your windows installs them. Call (972) 947-5677.

Can drapery go over existing shades?

Yes. Drapery layers over shades and blinds, and we plan the hardware so both work.

Do you supply the drapery or just install it?

Both. We quote fabric panels and hardware together with installation, so the lengths and stack widths are engineered rather than guessed.

Can drapery be motorized?

Yes. Motorized tracks glide panels from a remote, app or voice assistant, and they are quieter and smoother than most people expect.

How much do custom drapery cost in Irving?

It depends on the size of each opening, the material and the options you choose. We measure every opening on site and give you a written quote with installation included. The consultation is free.
