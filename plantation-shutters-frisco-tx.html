Shutters cannot absorb a crooked opening

Most of what goes wrong with window treatments is decided before anything is ordered: the wrong material for the room, or a mount the opening was never going to take.

A shutter is a rigid panel in a frame. Unlike a fabric shade it has no give, so an opening that is out of square has to be handled by the frame style rather than hidden by the treatment.

What changes the price

  • Material: hardwood, composite or vinyl
  • Louver size, which changes the view out and the material per panel
  • Frame style, and whether the opening is square enough for an inside fit
  • Panel configuration, since a wide window split four ways costs more than two
  • Specialty shapes: bays, arches and angles are built individually
  • Size of the opening, which drives material more than anything else

Do shutters add value to a home?
They stay with the house, so buyers read them as part of the property rather than a covering that will be replaced. That is why people put shutters in the rooms that show and blinds or shades elsewhere.
Real wood or composite?
Wood is lighter and takes stain beautifully, so it suits dry living areas. Composite handles moisture and hard sun without warping, which wins in bathrooms, kitchens and west facing walls.
How long do shutters take?
Longer than a stock blind, because they are built to your measurements. We confirm the timeline in writing on your quote.
Can shutters be painted later?
They can be refinished, but it is a job. Better to choose the finish carefully at the consultation, where you can hold samples against your own trim.
Do shutters block more light than blinds?
Closed louvers block more than a tilted slat, and a solid panel blocks more again. None of them are blackout unless the frame seals the edges.
