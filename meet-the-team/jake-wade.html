Jake Wade

Love Is Blinds North Texas

Jake Wade runs Love Is Blinds North Texas, one of the three Love Is Blinds franchises working across the state. That territory covers Sherman, Celina, Plano, McKinney, Denton and the northern suburbs.

Jake brings energy and adventure to everything he does. He loves being involved at his church, spending weekends on the softball field, and making memories with his nieces and nephew.

His outgoing personality and natural enthusiasm make him someone clients instantly feel comfortable with. Jake enjoys tackling challenges head-on and finding creative solutions that make life easier for the people he serves.
