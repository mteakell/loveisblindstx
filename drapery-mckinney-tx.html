Can drapery go over existing shades?
Yes. Drapery layers over shades and blinds, and we plan the hardware so both work.
Do you supply the drapery or just install it?
Both. We quote fabric panels and hardware together with installation, so the lengths and stack widths are engineered rather than guessed.
Can drapery be motorized?
Yes. Motorized tracks glide panels from a remote, app or voice assistant, and they are quieter and smoother than most people expect.
What does drapery add over blinds or shades?
Softness, height and finish. Most designed rooms layer drapery over a functional shade.
How high should drapery hang?
Mounting high adds height and extending wide keeps panels off the glass. We set rod height and bracket spacing at your windows.
