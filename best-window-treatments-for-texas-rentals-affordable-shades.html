Best Window Treatments for Rental Properties in Texas

Rental properties in Texas often come with basic, builder-grade blinds that warp, break, and don’t do much for privacy or heat control. The good news: you can upgrade the look and comfort of your space without turning your lease into a renovation project.

The key is choosing treatments that are durable, budget-friendly, and rental-appropriate, meaning they look polished, hold up to Texas sun, and can be installed and removed with minimal impact. At Love is Blinds, we help renters and landlords choose the right products and handle professional measuring and installation so everything fits correctly and looks intentional.

Ready-Made vs. Custom: What’s Best for Rentals?


Off-the-shelf shades can be tempting for the price, but rentals benefit most from a clean, precise fit, especially in bedrooms and street-facing rooms where light gaps and uneven coverage are obvious.

Ready-made: Works in a pinch, but sizing is limited and gaps are common.

Cut-to-size / custom-fit: Creates a tailored look, improves privacy and light control, and typically lasts longer, often the better value over time.

For rentals, custom-fit doesn’t have to mean “over-the-top.” It simply means the treatment fits the window correctly and is built to handle daily use.

You don't need a professional budget to fix those sloppy gaps. Learning "how to save money on custom blinds" comes down to using online or in-store "cut-to-width" services. This brilliant middle ground means a retailer takes a standard ready-made shade and mechanically trims the edges to your exact measurements, delivering a perfectly tailored look for just a few extra dollars.

When weighing "ready-made vs custom window treatments," consider this breakdown:

  1. Price: Ready-made is the cheapest; cut-to-width is a budget-friendly step up.
  2. Fit Precision: Shelf-stock leaves wide gaps; cut-to-width fits flush against the frame.
  3. Installation Time: Both take minutes to hang compared to complex full-custom hardware.

Regardless of which you choose, always watch for three signs of low-quality materials before buying: brittle plastic brackets, paper-thin fabrics, and uneven rolling tubes.


Cordless Roller Shades: Clean, Modern, and Renter-Friendly

Cordless roller shades are one of the most popular choices for rental spaces because they’re simple, sleek, and easy to live with. They also meet modern child-safety expectations by eliminating dangling cords.

Great for: Living rooms, offices, and any space where you want a streamlined look

Light control: Available in light-filtering, room-darkening, and blackout fabrics

Style bonus: Neutral fabrics instantly make a room feel more finished

Cellular (Honeycomb) Shades: A Smart Pick for Texas Heat

Texas sun is no joke. Cellular shades are designed with insulating pockets that help reduce heat transfer at the window, making rooms more comfortable and supporting HVAC efficiency.

Great for: Bedrooms, nurseries, west-facing windows, and energy-conscious tenants

Privacy: Excellent daytime privacy with light-filtering options

Comfort: Helps reduce hot spots and glare

Solar Shades: UV Protection Without Losing the View

If you have a bright living room or a great view, solar shades can be the perfect middle ground. They reduce glare and help protect floors and furniture from fading while still keeping the room open and airy.

Great for: Living rooms, dining rooms, and sun-heavy spaces

Best benefit: UV protection and glare control

Look: Modern and clean, especially in open-concept layouts

Blackout vs. Room-Darkening: What to Choose

Not every room needs full blackout. In rentals, it’s common to mix fabrics by room for comfort and budget.

Blackout: Best for bedrooms and shift-work sleepers

Room-darkening: A softer option that reduces light without making the room feel closed-in

Light-filtering: Ideal for living areas where you want privacy and natural light

Faux Wood Blinds: The Humidity-Proof Choice for Bathrooms

Real wood can warp in humidity, especially in bathrooms and laundry rooms. Faux wood blinds are a durable, moisture-resistant alternative that still delivers a classic, upscale look.

Great for: Bathrooms, kitchens, and any humid space

Maintenance: Easy to wipe clean

Durability: Built for everyday wear in rentals

Installation in Rentals: Keep It Lease-Friendly

Before changing window treatments, it’s smart to confirm any rules in your lease (or HOA guidelines, if applicable). For many rentals, the goal is a secure installation that looks professional and minimizes wall or trim impact.

Love is Blinds can recommend rental-friendly mounting approaches, match the right hardware to your window type, and ensure everything is aligned, level, and operating smoothly,without leaving you guessing.


A Simple Path to Upgrading Your Rental Windows

Step 1: Identify your top priority (privacy, heat control, glare reduction, blackout sleep).

Step 2: Choose the best style for each room (roller, cellular, solar, faux wood).

Step 3: Schedule professional measuring and installation so the fit and finish look right the first time.

If you’re ready for a cleaner look and better comfort, without the DIY headaches, Love is Blinds can help you find the best window treatments for rental properties in Texas and install them with care.

Frequently Asked Questions

Question: What window treatments look great in a rental without feeling “too permanent”?

Short answer: Cordless roller shades and cellular shades are top choices. They’re clean-looking, available in renter-friendly price ranges, and give a noticeably more polished finish than basic mini-blinds.

Question: Which option helps most with Texas heat and glare?

Short answer: Cellular shades help with insulation and comfort, while solar shades are excellent for glare and UV control,especially on sunny, west-facing windows.

Question: What’s best for bathrooms or other humid rooms?

Short answer: Faux wood blinds are a reliable, moisture-resistant choice and are easy to maintain,ideal for rentals.

Question: Do I need landlord approval before changing blinds or shades?

Short answer: Often, yes, especially if the existing treatments are considered part of the unit. We recommend checking your lease terms and getting written approval when needed. We can also help you choose options that align with common property guidelines.

Question: Can professional installation still be rental-friendly?

Short answer: Yes. Professional measuring and installation helps ensure the treatments fit properly, operate smoothly, and look consistent, while keeping the approach mindful of lease requirements.
