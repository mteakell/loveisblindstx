    Questions Texas homeowners ask us.

    Straight answers to what neighbors ask most. Have a different question? Call (866) 518-2999 and you'll talk to the owners.

    Do you really come to my home for the consultation?
    Yes, every time. Ben or Ashley brings the full sample case to your living room, measures each window, and writes up an honest quote on the spot. You see the blinds, shades, and shutters in your own light before you decide anything.
    What areas of Texas do you serve?
    Everywhere within about a two-hour radius of Fort Worth - the Greater Fort Worth area, Dallas and Ringgold, the Calhoun-Cartersville-Rome corridor, the Blue Ridge and Ellijay mountains. If you don't see your town on our service areas page, call us - we probably cover it.
    How much do custom blinds, shades, or plantation shutters cost?
    It depends on the size and number of windows, the material, and any specialty shapes. Since everything is custom-made to your openings, the honest way to get a real number is a free in-home consultation, where we measure and hand you an itemized quote with no obligation.
    Which window treatments handle Texas heat and humidity best?
    Faux wood blinds and composite shutters shrug off humidity in kitchens and baths, solar shades tame the brutal western sun, and honeycomb shades insulate against both July heat and January cold snaps. The in-home consultation matches the right material to each room.
    How long does it take from consultation to install?
    Most projects run two to four weeks from consultation to install day. Custom plantation shutters can run longer because they're built to your exact frames. You get the timeline in writing before anything is ordered.
    Do you offer motorized or smart shades?
    Yes. Motorized blinds and shades run from a remote, your phone, or a quick word to Alexa, Google, or Siri - perfect for tall or hard-to-reach windows. We program and test everything on install day.
