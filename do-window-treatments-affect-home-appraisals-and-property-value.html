Do Window Treatments Affect Home Appraisals and Property Value?

Yes, window treatments can impact a home’s appraisal and property value, but the extent depends on factors like quality, functionality, and design. While appraisers primarily assess structural elements, high-quality, custom window treatments can enhance aesthetics, energy efficiency, and privacy,features that indirectly increase home desirability and selling potential.

Understanding Home Appraisals and Value Determination

A home appraisal is a professional assessment of a property’s worth, conducted by a licensed appraiser. This valuation is crucial for refinancing, selling, or securing a mortgage. Appraisers consider multiple factors, including location, square footage, renovations, and permanent fixtures.

While window treatments are often seen as decor rather than a structural element, they can influence an appraiser’s overall perception of the home’s upkeep and modernity. Permanent, high-end treatments like plantation shutters, custom blinds, and energy-efficient shades can contribute to a higher perceived value.

The Role of Window Treatments in Property Value

What Are Window Treatments?


Window treatments encompass a variety of coverings used to enhance privacy, light control, and aesthetics. These include:

  • Blinds – Slatted coverings made of wood, faux wood, metal, or vinyl.
  • Shades – Fabric-based window coverings that roll or fold up.
  • Shutters – Solid structures, often made of wood or composite, that provide insulation and security.
  • Drapes & Curtains – Fabric panels used for decorative and functional purposes.

How Do They Impact Property Value?


  1. Aesthetic Appeal – Modern and well-maintained window treatments that add value contribute to a polished, updated look that appeals to buyers.
  2. Energy Efficiency – Insulated shades, thermal curtains, and cellular blinds can reduce heating and cooling costs, making the home more attractive.
  3. Privacy & Security – High-quality treatments enhance privacy, a key selling point for homeowners.
  4. Curb Appeal – Exterior-facing window treatments, like shutters, add to a home’s overall charm and first impression.

Best Window Coverings for Property Value

When choosing window treatments, it's essential to consider their impact on home value. If your home features expansive openings, selecting the right window treatments for large glass doors can enhance both functionality and aesthetic appeal while contributing to energy efficiency.

Window Treatment Impact on Home Value Pros Cons
Blinds Moderate Affordable, versatile, light control Can appear basic if not high quality
Shades Moderate-High Energy-efficient, sleek design Less durable than shutters
Shutters High Permanent fixture, enhances curb appeal Higher upfront cost
Drapes Low-Moderate Style variety, easy to replace date

Among these, plantation shutters and energy-efficient cellular shades are the best investments for boosting property value.

Energy Efficiency & Cost Savings: A Hidden Value Factor

Energy-efficient window treatments can provide significant savings on utility bills. According to the U.S. Department of Energy, energy-efficient window coverings can reduce heat gain in summer by up to 77% and heat loss in winter by 40%.

Popular energy-efficient treatments include:

  • Cellular (Honeycomb) Shades – Trap air in pockets to insulate windows.
  • Solar Shades – Reduce UV rays while maintaining natural light.
  • Thermal Curtains – Prevent heat loss, ideal for colder climates.
  • Shutters with Insulation – Provide year-round energy savings.

These cost-saving benefits can make a home more attractive to potential buyers looking for efficiency and sustainability.

Window Treatments & Home Appraisals: What Appraisers Consider

Home appraisers primarily focus on permanent features that affect a property’s marketability. While standard blinds or temporary curtains don’t add appraisal value, custom, built-in window treatments like plantation shutters can be considered an asset.

Custom vs. Standard Window Treatments

Type Considered in Appraisal? Value Impact
Standard Blinds No Minimal
Drapes & Curtains No Only aesthetic
Custom Blinds & Shades Possibly Enhances appeal
Plantation Shutters Yes Considered a permanent fixture

A well-maintained home with high-end treatments signals attention to detail, which may result in a higher perceived home value.

Regional & Market Trends: What Homebuyers Want

Different regions favor specific window treatments based on climate and buyer preferences:

  • Hot Climates (Texas, Florida, Arizona) – Weather-resistant window treatments like solar shades and blackout curtains help block heat while protecting interiors from sun damage.
  • Cold Climates (Northeast, Midwest) – Insulated drapes and cellular shades to retain warmth.
  • Luxury Markets – Custom plantation shutters or motorized blinds for added sophistication.

Conclusion & Takeaways

So, do window treatments affect home appraisals and property value? While they may not be a primary factor in appraisals, high-quality, energy-efficient, and custom window treatments can enhance home appeal, improve energy savings, and ultimately boost resale value.

Key Takeaways:


  • Permanent window treatments like shutters add more value than temporary ones.
  • Energy-efficient coverings lower costs and appeal to eco-conscious buyers.
  • Homes with high-end window treatments can sell faster and attract more offers.
