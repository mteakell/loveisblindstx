## Why these and not the full matrix

The full grid of 6 products x 47 cities is 282 pages. Only 64 combinations have any
search volume at all, so 218 of them, 77 percent, would target zero searches. That is
the doorway-page pattern and is not worth the risk to the rest of the site.

Deliberately not built:

| Set | Volume | Why not |
|---|---|---|
| `blinds-{city}` | 1,290/mo | Already what the 48 city pages target in their own H1 |
| `shutters-{city}` | 230/mo | Same intent as the plantation shutter pages, would cannibalise |
| `shades-{city}` | 280/mo | Spread thin over 8 cities, and the city pages already carry shades |
| everything else | 0/mo | No demand |

## Rules used

1. A page only exists where the keyword has measured volume.
2. Every page carries its own operator name, local phone, nearby cities, price drivers and FAQ.
3. Linked both ways: the product page lists its cities, each city page links to its own.
4. Full schema per page: LocalBusiness, Service with OfferCatalog, FAQPage, BreadcrumbList, WebPage.
