Panel Track Shades for Frisco homes

A Frisco great room often has a long run of glass at the back, sometimes with transoms above. A single panel track can run that full span, and wide panels keep a large wall from looking busy. The transoms above can stay open to daylight or get their own treatment, planned in the same visit.

Media and game rooms with a door to the yard usually want the blackout end of the fabric range. We plan the whole house, and the great room's panel track, the media room and the bedrooms each get the right fabric and still look related.

Panel Track Shades questions from Frisco

Can a panel track shade cover the wide back wall of glass in a Frisco great room?

Yes. One track can run the full span. We measure the opening in your Frisco home and set the panel count so the stack clears the door.

Do blackout panel track shades work in a Frisco media room?

When the media room has a slider or wide window, full blackout panel track fabric covers it and stacks to the side whenever the door is open.

Do you install panel track shades in Frisco?

Yes. Jake Wade and Jonathan Arosemena run Love Is Blinds North Texas, which covers Frisco along with Dallas, Highland Park, Irving, Las Colinas, Plano, McKinney, Prosper, Celina, Sherman and the surrounding suburbs. The team that measures your windows installs them. Call (469) 707-9424.

Do panel tracks rattle like verticals?

No. The panels are broad and flat and move quietly on the track.

What are people replacing vertical blinds with?

Mostly panel tracks: the same wide-opening coverage with a far better look and durability. Sheer verticals and drapery are the alternatives.

Do panel tracks work on a standard patio slider?

Yes, that is their most common job. We size the track and panel count so the stack clears the walkway side.
