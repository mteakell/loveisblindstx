Woven Wood Shades for Celina homes

A blackout liner turns a woven wood shade into a bedroom shade that covers like any other. In a Celina primary bedroom, that means bamboo or jute texture on the room side and a dark room at sunrise. Some early light can slip past the edges of any shade, and drapery layered over the woven shade is the extra step for those gaps.

Windows on both sides of the bed should match. We measure each one on site and build each shade to it, which keeps the folds level and the two shades reading as a pair. One quote covers every bedroom, installed by the same team that measured.

Woven Wood Shades questions from Celina

Will a woven wood shade keep my east-facing Celina bedroom dark at sunrise?

With a blackout liner, it covers the glass like any blackout shade. If light at the edges bothers you, drapery over the shade closes those gaps.

Can the woven wood shades on both sides of my Celina bed match?

Yes, both are built in the same weave from the measurements we take at each window. Natural materials vary slightly, which gives each shade a subtly unique pattern.

Do you install woven wood shades in Celina?

Yes. Jake Wade and Jonathan Arosemena run Love Is Blinds North Texas, which covers Celina along with Dallas, Highland Park, Irving, Las Colinas, Plano, Frisco, McKinney, Prosper, Sherman and the surrounding suburbs. The team that measures your windows installs them. Call (972) 846-9683.

Where do woven wood shades work best?

Sunrooms, dining rooms, offices, and farmhouse, lake and ranch houses where natural texture suits the room.

Are woven shades good for rentals?

With cordless lifts, yes. There are no cords for guests to damage.

Do woven shades change in the sun?

Natural materials can mellow in strong sun over time. We look at each window's exposure when you choose a weave.

How much do woven wood shades cost in Celina?

It depends on the size of each opening, the material and the options you choose. We measure every opening on site and give you a written quote with installation included. The consultation is free.
