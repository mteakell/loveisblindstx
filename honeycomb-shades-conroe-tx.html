Honeycomb Shades questions from Conroe

When should I measure for honeycomb shades in a new Conroe build?

Each opening is measured on site, so measuring works best once the windows and trim are in place. We can plan the whole house in that one visit.

Do I need double-cell honeycomb shades in a new Conroe home?

Not always. Newer double-pane windows often do fine with single-cell. We suggest double-cell where the glass is large and spec each window separately.

Do you install honeycomb shades in Conroe?

Yes. Love Is Blinds Texas covers Conroe along with Magnolia, Tomball, The Woodlands, Spring, Montgomery, Willis, Waller and the communities between them. The team that measures your windows installs them. Call (281) 864-3309.

How do I clean cellular shades?

A vacuum brush or a hair dryer on cool blows dust out of the cells. Spot clean with a barely damp cloth.

Single cell or double cell?

Double cell insulates more and costs more. It earns its keep on big glass and older single-pane windows; double-pane windows in a newer build often do fine with single cell. We spec it window by window.

Are honeycomb and cellular shades the same thing?

Yes. Both names describe fabric folded into hollow cells that trap air at the window.

How much do honeycomb shades cost in Conroe?

It depends on the size of each opening, the material and the options you choose. We measure every opening on site and give you a written quote with installation included. The consultation is free.
