Roller shades

A consistent look from outside

Some associations ask that window treatments look uniform from the street or courtyard. Roller shades make that easy, because one fabric color can run across every window in the unit. A light filtering fabric keeps the daylight while blocking the view in from the building across the way.

See roller shades
02
Panel track shades

Balcony and patio doors

The sliding door to the balcony gets used every day, so its treatment has to stack clear of the opening. Panel track shades glide wide fabric panels along a track and park to one side, and they replace vertical blinds that swing and rattle when the air conditioning comes on.

For a door opened only now and then, a roller shade keeps the look minimal.

See panel track shades
03
Blackout roller shades

Bedrooms facing lights at night

Street lights, parking lots and the windows next door keep a bedroom bright long after dark. Blackout fabric stops the light that hits the shade itself; what gets in is the thin line at each edge. Side channels, or an outside mount that overlaps the frame, close those gaps.

Questions

Las Colinas window treatment questions

Do you install window treatments in Las Colinas condos?

Yes, in condos, townhomes and apartments as well as houses. If your association reviews changes, we can give you the product details it asks for.

What replaces vertical blinds on a balcony door?

Panel track shades are the most common choice. They stack to one side and move the same way the door does.

How dark can a bedroom get with city lights outside?

Very dark with blackout fabric and side channels. Without them, a thin line of light shows at the edges.

Is Las Colinas covered by the same team as Irving?

Yes. One Dallas-area team serves both, and the consultation is free.

Can I keep one color across the whole unit?

Yes. We choose fabrics in the same color family for every window and door, which keeps the outside view uniform.

How much do window treatments cost in Las Colinas?

It depends on the size of each opening, the material and the options you choose. We measure every opening on site and give you a written quote with installation included. The consultation is free.
