Custom Blinds for Corsicana homes

An inside mount sits within the window frame and shows off the trim, but it needs enough depth for the headrail and a frame that is close to square. An outside mount covers the frame, hides an uneven opening and blocks more light at the edges. In Corsicana homes we measure the depth and squareness of every opening before recommending one.

Stock blinds often fail at exactly this point, with a bracket that does not suit a shallow frame. A custom blind is built for the mount you choose, from measurements we take.

All about custom blinds
  • Blackout blinds for bedroomsA tighter slat and an outside mount with overlap, because an inside mount always leaks at the edges.
  • Real wood blindsHardwood slats in stains and paints matched to your trim. The warmest looking option and the lightest to lift.
  • Faux wood blindsMoisture resistant and dimensionally stable. The default for bathrooms, kitchens and any window taking hard afternoon sun.
  • Composite blindsSplits the difference on cost and durability, with a finish closer to real wood than vinyl.
  • Vertical blindsFor sliding doors and wide openings where a horizontal blind would be unwieldy.
  • Mini and micro blindsSlim slats for small openings, utility spaces and rentals.

Custom Blinds questions from Corsicana

Should blinds in my Corsicana home be inside or outside mounted?

Inside if the frame is deep enough and square, to show the trim. Outside if the frame is shallow or uneven, or if you want more coverage at the edges.

How deep does a Corsicana window frame need to be for an inside-mount blind?

It depends on the slat size and the headrail. We measure each frame on site and tell you which mount it supports.

Do you install custom blinds in Corsicana?

Yes. Love Is Blinds at Cedar Creek Lake covers Corsicana along with Gun Barrel City, Kerens and the Cedar Creek Lake communities. The team that measures your windows installs them. Call (903) 420-9161.

Can you match blinds to existing trim?

Usually. Bring us a sample or we will take one at the consultation and match the stain or paint against it.

How long do custom blinds last?

With the right material for the room, a decade or more. What shortens that is putting real wood in a bathroom or vinyl on a west facing window.

Can I get blinds for an arched window?

Yes, though arches are templated on site rather than measured from numbers, and the arch itself is usually fixed rather than operable.

How much do custom blinds cost in Corsicana?

It depends on the size of each opening, the material and the options you choose. We measure every opening on site and give you a written quote with installation included. The consultation is free.
