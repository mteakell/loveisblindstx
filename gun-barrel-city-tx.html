Roller and solar shades

Solar shades that slow fading

A solar shade is a woven fabric that cuts glare and UV at the glass while you still see through it. On the water side of the house, the view stays and the sun that bleaches wood and upholstery is filtered before it reaches them.

A tighter weave filters more; a more open one shows more of the lake.

Motorized window treatments

A schedule for a house that sits empty some days

If the house is a weekend place, or everyone is out on the water all afternoon, nobody is home to lower the shades when the sun comes around. Motorized shades can run on a schedule, down when the sun reaches the water side and up again in the evening. Battery motors need no new wiring, and a whole room can share one setting.

See motorized window treatments
03
Exterior patio shades

Shade for the deck and porch

Decks and covered porches that face the water take the same sun as the living room behind them. Exterior shades mount to the porch header and stop that sun before it reaches the chairs or the glass. Retractable versions roll up out of wind and hail when the porch is empty.

Questions

Gun Barrel City window treatment questions

Will solar shades stop my floors from fading?

They cut the UV that causes fading at the glass, which slows it down. Rooms with the most sun do best when the shades also close on a schedule.

Can motorized shades run when nobody is home?

Yes. A schedule raises and lowers them at set times, and battery motors keep working from the remote even if the Wi-Fi goes out.

Do you install shades on lake porches and decks?

Yes. Exterior shades mount to the porch or deck header, and a motor is the quickest way to get them up when the wind turns.
