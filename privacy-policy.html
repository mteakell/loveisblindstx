Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information loveisblindstx.com collects, what it is used for, and which services handle it. It is written to match what the site actually runs.

Who we are

Love Is Blinds Texas is a group of locally owned Love Is Blinds locations serving DFW, North Texas, Dallas, Waco, Austin and the Cedar Creek Lake area, sharing this website. When you contact us, your request goes to the location that serves your city.

Information you give us

  • Consultation forms: your first and last name, phone number, email address, city, ZIP code, the products you are interested in, and anything you choose to write in the message.
  • Phone calls: when you call a number shown on this site, our call tracking service records your phone number, the time and length of the call, and which page or ad led to the call. Calls may be recorded for quality and training.
  • Website chat: if you use the chat assistant on the site, the messages you type are collected so we can answer you.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, this site collects standard usage information: the pages you visit, how you arrived (for example a search or an ad), your device and browser type, and your approximate location based on your IP address. This is collected through the analytics and advertising tools listed below, some of which use cookies.

How we use it

  • To respond to your request, schedule your in-home consultation, prepare your quote, and install and service your window treatments
  • To understand how the site is used and improve it
  • To measure whether our advertising leads to consultation requests

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not rent or share it with anyone outside the business for their own marketing.

Services that process your information

  • Formspree delivers consultation form submissions to us.
  • CallRail provides call tracking for the phone numbers shown on the site.
  • Venbit provides site analytics and the website chat assistant.
  • Google provides Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, Google Ads conversion measurement, Google Fonts and the Google Maps embeds on our location pages.
  • Vercel hosts the site, keeps standard server logs such as IP addresses, and provides Vercel Web Analytics, which measures page views without cookies.

Each of these services handles data under its own privacy policy.

Cookies and advertising

Google Analytics uses cookies to count visits and understand how the site is used. When you arrive from one of our Google ads, Google Ads stores an ad click identifier in a cookie so we can tell whether the ad led to a consultation request. Google Maps and other embeds may set their own cookies.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, opt out of Google Analytics with Google's opt-out add-on, and manage ad personalization in Google's My Ad Center.

Text messages

If you give us your mobile number and agree to receive text messages, we use it only to contact you about your request, consultation, order or installation. You can reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts, or HELP for help.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are excluded from every category of sharing described in this policy and will not be shared with any third parties.

How long we keep it

We keep consultation requests and customer records for as long as they are useful for serving you, including warranty and service needs, and you can ask us to delete yours at any time.

Your choices

You can ask what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email access@loveisblindstx.com or call (866) 518-2999. If you prefer not to use the forms, every page lists a phone number.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect the information you send us, and the site is served only over encrypted HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure.

Children

This site is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes

When what the site collects changes, this page changes with it. This policy was last updated on November 4, 2026. See also our Terms of Service.
