Blackout fabric and the edges

Blackout fabric blocks essentially all light. An inside-mounted shade still leaves a small gap at each side, which shows as a line of light in a dark room. Side channels close those gaps on roller shades, and an outside mount that overlaps the frame closes most of it on any shade.

Drapery over a shade

For the darkest room, layer drapery over a working shade. The shade does the daily work and the drapery closes the edges and adds softness. Motorized drapery tracks move the panels from a remote or a schedule.

Cellular blackout for heat too

A blackout cellular shade adds insulation to the darkness, which helps a west-facing bedroom stay cooler in the afternoon.

Bedrooms, nurseries and media rooms

The same planning works anywhere you need a dark room. A media room needs the screen free of glare during the day; a nursery needs darkness for naps and a cordless lift for safety. Blackout roller shades with side channels handle both, and drapery adds the finishing layer.

Shenandoah window treatment questions

How do I get a bedroom completely dark?
Blackout fabric plus a way to close the edges: side channels, an outside mount with overlap, or drapery over the shade.
Do blackout roller shades leak light?
The fabric blocks essentially all light. The edges are what leak on an inside mount.
Can drapery be motorized?
Yes. Motorized tracks move panels from a remote, app, voice assistant or schedule.
Which shade is best for a nursery?
A cordless blackout shade, ideally with the edges closed by side channels or an outside mount.
Can a media room be made dark during the day?
Yes. Blackout roller shades with side channels close the edge gaps, and drapery over them darkens the room further.
How much do window treatments cost in Shenandoah?
It depends on the size of each opening, the material and the options you choose. We measure every opening on site and give you a written quote with installation included. The consultation is free.
