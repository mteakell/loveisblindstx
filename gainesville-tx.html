Gainesville questions, answered

Do you offer free in-home consultations in Gainesville?
Yes. We bring samples to your home in Gainesville, measure every opening ourselves and quote from those measurements. There is no charge for the visit and no obligation to order. Call (940) 236-2599 to book a time.
How long does an order take in Gainesville?
Most custom orders arrive within two to four weeks of approval, depending on the product line and fabric. Motorized treatments can run longer. We confirm the timeline in writing on your quote.
Who measures and installs in Gainesville?
Jake Wade and Jonathan Arosemena run Love Is Blinds North Texas, which covers Gainesville along with Sherman, Celina, Plano, McKinney, Denton and the northern suburbs. The person who measures your windows is the person who fits them.
Do you install exterior patio shades in Gainesville?
Yes. Exterior patio shades, outdoor roller shades and motorized patio screens are a large part of what we do in Gainesville, because shading the outside of the glass is far more effective against Texas afternoon sun than an interior blind on the same opening.
What if a treatment does not fit correctly?
We measured it, so we correct it. If an opening is wrong against the approved measurements, we remake it and reinstall at no cost to you.
