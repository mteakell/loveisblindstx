Window treatments for Gainesville homes

An original window in an older house is often single-pane glass in a frame that has moved with the house. In January you feel cold air beside it, and in August the same glass passes heat straight into the room. Bedrooms and the rooms you sit in for hours are where people notice it first.

At the consultation our DFW team looks at each window's glass and fit, measures every opening on site, and plans layers that insulate. The quote is written with installation included, and we remove the old blinds at installation.

Honeycomb shades

Double cell shades for single-pane glass

Cellular shades hold a pocket of air against the glass, which slows heat moving through it in both directions. Double cell construction adds a second layer of cells, and it earns its cost on older single-pane windows. Top-down bottom-up operation lets daylight in at the top while the lower glass stays covered.

Drapery

Drapery over the shade in the coldest rooms

For a bedroom that runs cold, full drapery over a cellular shade puts a second layer between you and the glass. Panels hung high and wide stack off the window during the day and close across it at night, and a motorized track can open and close them on a schedule.

See drapery
03
Plantation shutters

Shutters that close against drafts

Plantation shutters are built in their own frame, so an older opening that is out of square still gets a close fit. Closed, the solid panels insulate against winter drafts and summer heat better than any blind. On a mild day, tilt the louvers and the light comes back in.

Questions

Gainesville window treatment questions

What helps most with a drafty window in an older Gainesville home?

A double cell cellular shade at the glass, with drapery or shutters added in the rooms that run coldest. We look at each window before recommending a layer.

Do I need single cell or double cell shades?

Double cell for older single-pane windows and large glass. Single cell often does the job on newer double-pane windows.

Will shutters fit an older window frame?

Yes. Each shutter is built in its own frame, and we choose the frame style from the measurements we take at your house.

Is Gainesville served by a local team?

Yes. Our DFW team handles Gainesville consultations, measuring and installation, and the people who measure are the people who install.

Should I replace my windows before adding new treatments?

We measure and build to the openings you have today. If new windows are planned soon, install those first so the measurements match the new frames.

How much do window treatments cost in Gainesville?

It depends on the size of each opening, the material and the options you choose. We measure every opening on site and give you a written quote with installation included. The consultation is free.
