Older homes are rarely square

The consultation happens in your home, with samples held against your own light, because a fabric that looks right in a showroom often looks wrong on a west wall at four in the afternoon.

Frames settle. On most homes past a few years old the width at the top of a window is not the width at the bottom, which is why we measure in three places and often recommend an outside mount even where an inside mount would look neater.

Blackout blinds for bedrooms

Real wood blinds

Hardwood slats in stains and paints matched to your trim. The warmest looking option and the lightest to lift.

Faux wood blinds

Moisture resistant and dimensionally stable. The default for bathrooms, kitchens and any window taking hard afternoon sun.

Composite blinds

Splits the difference on cost and durability, with a finish closer to real wood than vinyl.

Vertical blinds

For sliding doors and wide openings where a horizontal blind would be unwieldy.

What changes the price

  • Size of the opening, which drives material more than anything else
  • Material: real wood, composite, faux wood or vinyl
  • Slat width, which changes the view out and the stack at the top
  • Inside or outside mount, and whether the frame is deep and square enough
  • Cordless, motorized or standard lift

Custom Blinds questions from North Richland Hills

Do you install custom blinds in North Richland Hills?
Yes. Durrell Glick runs Love Is Blinds DFW, which covers North Richland Hills along with Fort Worth, the Mid-Cities, Southlake, Grapevine, Coppell and Granbury. The person who measures your windows is the person who fits them. Call (817) 587-6007.
Are cordless blinds standard now?
Yes, on most new blinds, for child and pet safety. It is worth confirming any quote includes it, because an unusually low price sometimes still assumes a corded lift.
Can you match blinds to existing trim?
Usually. Bring us a sample or we will take one at the consultation and match the stain or paint against it.
How long do custom blinds last?
With the right material for the room, a decade or more. What shortens that is putting real wood in a bathroom or vinyl on a west facing window.
Can I get blinds for an arched window?
Yes, though arches are templated on site rather than measured from numbers, and the arch itself is usually fixed rather than operable.
Do you remove the old blinds?
Yes, and we take them away if you want them gone. There is no separate charge for it.
How much do custom blinds cost in North Richland Hills?
It depends on the size of the openings, the material and the options you choose. We measure every opening on site and quote from those measurements, so the number on your estimate is the number you pay. The consultation is free.
